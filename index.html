Black Friday questions, answered

When does the OneLaser Black Friday sale run?

Nov 10 – Nov 30, 2026, ending at 11:59 PM Pacific Time. Early Access subscribers can shop SUPER Deals 24 hours early.

Do I need a discount code?

No. All Black Friday prices are final prices and apply automatically. Discount codes, including creator codes, can't be combined with Black Friday prices.

How does the Hydra 07 Pro "first 14" deal work?

The first 14 Hydra 07 Pro (Gen2) units sell at $7,999. Once those 14 are sold, the price moves to $8,999 for the remaining units. The live counter on this page shows how many of the first 14 are left.

When will my machine ship?
  • In stock · Ships from US — ships from our US warehouse.
  • In transit — on its way to the US; the expected ship date is shown on the product.
What's the difference between the two XRF 38W RF bundles?

Starter Bundle ($4,499): XRF 38W RF, free Air Assist box and free shipping. Maker Bundle ($4,999): everything in the Starter Bundle plus a base and 2 material packs (acrylic + leather).

Are accessories and materials on sale?

Black Friday pricing applies to the laser machines shown on this page. Accessories and materials are at regular prices.

Can I pay over time?

Yes. Businesses can apply for lease-to-own financing with Clicklease. Approval and terms are set by the financing provider.

What if I change my mind?

OneLaser offers a 30-day return policy and official warranty coverage. See our return policy for details.

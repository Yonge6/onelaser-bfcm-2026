When does the sale run?
Black Friday Deals are live now and run through Cyber Monday, Nov 30, 2026, ending at 11:59 PM Pacific Time. Prices are the same every day of the sale, but stock is limited and any deal can sell out before then.

Starter Bundle ($4,499): XRF 38W RF, a free Air Assist Control and free shipping. Maker Bundle ($4,999): everything in the Starter Bundle plus a riser base and two material packs (acrylic and leather). Both are the same machine.

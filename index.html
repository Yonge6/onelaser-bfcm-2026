FAQ

Questions before you buy

Which machines are on sale?
Super Deals: XRF 38W RF, Hydra 07 Pro (Gen2) and Cobra 08. BFCM Deals: VertiGo, Cobra 10 and 14, Hydra 09 Dual and the Hydra Gen2 Pro line (09, 13 and 16). Plus single last units of the Hydra 07 80W and Hydra 16 Dual. Hydra Gen2 Hybrid stays at its regular price. Accessories, materials and services are not discounted.
When does the sale run?
Nov 10 to Nov 30, 2026, ending at 11:59 PM Pacific Time. Early access subscribers can shop the Super Deals 24 hours early. Prices do not return in December.
Do I need a discount code?
No. Black Friday prices are final and apply automatically. Discount codes, including creator codes, are paused during the sale and cannot be combined with Black Friday prices.
How does the Hydra 07 Pro price work?
The first 14 units sell at $7,999 (MSRP $10,999, save $3,000). The moment unit 14 sells, the price moves to $8,999 for the remaining units. The counter on the product card shows how many of the first 14 are left.
What is the difference between the two XRF bundles?
Starter Bundle ($4,499): XRF 38W RF, a free Air Assist box and free shipping. Maker Bundle ($4,999): everything in the Starter Bundle plus a riser base and two material packs (acrylic and leather). Both are the same machine.
What does "in stock" and "arriving" mean?
  • In stock: the unit is in our Lake Forest, California warehouse and ships from there.
  • Arriving: the unit is on its way to the U.S. Your order is a backorder that ships from the next shipment, in order of purchase. See the preorder and backorder policy.
Why is a clearance machine cheaper, and is it still supported?
Clearance models are being retired from the catalog to make room for the Gen2 line. They are new, boxed machines with full warranty, technical support and parts. The only thing that changes is that we will not build more of them.
What payment options are available?
Card, PayPal and the other methods on our payment methods page. Businesses can apply for lease to own financing through Clicklease; approval, rates and terms are set by the financing provider.
What if I change my mind?
OneLaser offers a 30 day return policy and official warranty coverage on every machine, including Black Friday orders. See the refund policy for details.
I am not sure which machine is right for me.
Use the three question finder above, then book a free call with a U.S. based laser expert. They will match a machine to your materials, volume and space.

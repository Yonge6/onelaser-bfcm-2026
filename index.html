FAQ

Which products are included in the Black Friday Sale?
XRF 38W RF, Hydra 07 Pro (Gen2), Hydra Gen2 Pro, Hydra 09 Dual, Cobra 08 / 10 / 14 and VertiGo, plus final units of select discontinued models. Hydra Gen2 Hybrid is sold at its regular price. Accessories, materials and services are at regular prices.
When does the OneLaser Black Friday sale run?
Nov 10 – Nov 30, 2026, ending at 11:59 PM Pacific Time. Early Access subscribers can shop SUPER Deals 24 hours early.
Do I need a discount code?
No. Black Friday prices are final prices and apply automatically. Discount codes, including creator codes, can't be combined with Black Friday prices.
Which product can save up to $3,000 during the sale?
Hydra 07 Pro (Gen2). The first 14 units sell at $7,999 (MSRP $10,999). Once those 14 are sold, the price moves to $8,999 for the remaining units.
What's the difference between the two XRF 38W RF bundles?
Starter Bundle ($4,499): XRF 38W RF, free Air Assist box and free shipping. Maker Bundle ($4,999): everything in the Starter Bundle plus a base and 2 material packs (acrylic + leather).
When will my machine ship?
  • In stock — ships from our US warehouse.
  • In transit — on its way to the US; the expected ship date is shown on the product.
What payment options are available?
Businesses can apply for lease-to-own financing with Clicklease. Approval, rates and terms are set by the financing provider.
I'm not sure which OneLaser machine is right for me. What should I do?
Take our quick quiz, or book a free sales consultation call with a U.S.-based laser expert.
What if I change my mind?
OneLaser offers a 30-day return policy and official warranty coverage.
